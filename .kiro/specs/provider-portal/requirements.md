# Requirements: Provider portal

Traces to: BR-9

## User story
As a provider, I see who I have supported and who needs what I offer, and keep my listing current.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
1. THE SYSTEM SHALL let a provider access only records of their own organization.
2. WHEN a seeker has agreed to share THE SYSTEM SHALL show the provider first name, ZIP code and need, but not contact details.
3. WHEN a provider offers support THE SYSTEM SHALL reveal the seeker's chosen contact details.
4. THE SYSTEM SHALL show unassigned needs whose text matches the provider's services.
5. WHEN a provider edits a record THE SYSTEM SHALL send it for admin review.
