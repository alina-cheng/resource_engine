# Product
One Degree Resource Engine: ingests community-resource data (ODRS, 211 NDP), converts it to Open Referral HSDS 3.2.3, keeps it accurate with an AI maintenance worker supervised by humans, and serves help seekers, providers and One Degree admins.
Principles: accuracy over speed; a person approves every change to live data; the AI proposes with evidence, never applies; seekers control what is shared; failures are findings, never silent skips.
Personas: help seeker, help provider, One Degree admin, engineer/data partner. Requirements are in `docs/BRD.md` (BR-1..BR-15).
